# LIVE-VID-001 - Other Participants' Video Feeds Appear Black During Live Session on Android

* **Severity:** High
* **Environment:** Android 14 · Red Note 14S · Staging · v1.21.0
* **Reproducibility:** 80%

## Summary
When joining a live session from an Android device, other participants' video feeds frequently fail to load and appear completely black despite their cameras being turned on.

## Steps
1. Open the mobile application on the Android device.
2. Join an active live session.
3. Observe the video feeds of other participants whose cameras are enabled.

## Actual
Other participants' video feeds appear completely black even though their cameras are on.

## Expected
Other participants' video feeds should load and display properly during the live session.

## Notes
Subcategory: Functional Completeness / Correctness. Intermittent (80%) remote video stream rendering or media synchronization issue on Android.
