# LIVE-TMR-001 - "Add Time" Button Does Not Respond During Participant's Round

* **Severity:** Medium
* **Environment:** Android 14 · Red Note 14S · Staging · v1.21.0
* **Reproducibility:** 70%

## Summary
During a live debate session, while the participant's own speaking/debate round is active, tapping the "Add Time" (time request) button fails to respond in 70% of attempts.

## Steps
1. Join a live session and start/enter your active debate round.
2. Tap the "Add Time" (time request) button.
3. Observe whether the button reacts or opens the time-extension option.

## Actual
The "Add Time" (time request) button does not respond to the tap.

## Expected
The "Add Time" button should be interactive during the participant's active round and allow adding/requesting additional time.

## Notes
Subcategory: Functional Appropriateness. Intermittent (70%) responsiveness issue affecting an in-session time control during an active round.
