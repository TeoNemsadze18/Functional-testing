# WEB-SKP-001 - "Skip" Button Not Visible at Start of Participant's Round on Web

* **Severity:** Medium
* **Environment:** Web · MacBook Air (M1, 2020) · macOS Sonoma 14.4 · Staging · v1.21.0
* **Reproducibility:** 100%

## Summary
In the Web version, when the participant's round starts during a live debate session, the "Skip" button is not visible on screen.

## Steps
1. Sign in to the Web application and join a live debate session.
2. Wait for your speaking/debate round to start.
3. Check whether the "Skip" button is visible.

## Actual
When your round starts, the "Skip" button is not visible.

## Expected
When your round starts, the "Skip" button should be visible and accessible.

## Notes
Platform: Web. Functional Completeness / Appropriateness: expected round control is missing or hidden on the Web client at round start.
